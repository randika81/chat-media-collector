Chat Media Collector Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Published by randika81

In short: Chat Media Collector has no servers and collects nothing. Your Telegram sign-in, your settings and the photos and videos you save stay on your PC. The app talks only to Telegram (to sign in and download the media you ask for) and to the Microsoft Store (for your licence).

What the app does

Chat Media Collector signs in to your own Telegram account and saves the photos and videos of the chats and channels you pick into folders on your PC, in the order they were posted. It is an unofficial app, not made by or affiliated with Telegram.

Information that stays on your PC

  • Your Telegram sign-in. After you sign in, Telegram gives the app a session so you don't have to sign in again. It is saved in the app's own folder, encrypted. Your phone number, if you signed in with it, is saved encrypted with Windows' data protection for your Windows account only.
  • Your settings, such as where chat folders go and which chats are kept in sync.
  • Activity history: which chats were collected, when, and how many files (up to the last 300).
  • Chat photos (the small pictures in the chat list), kept so the list loads quickly.
  • A small technical log of connection warnings and errors, up to 1 MB, to help with problems. It isn't sent anywhere.
  • The photos and videos you save, in the folders you choose.

If you use Unigram, the app can read Unigram's download folder on your PC to reuse files it already has, instead of downloading them again. Those files are read locally and never uploaded.

Information sent to others

  • Telegram. To sign you in and to list and download media, the app connects directly to Telegram's servers, as any Telegram app does. What you enter to sign in (your phone number, the login code, your email code or your two-step verification password) goes to Telegram only. Telegram's own privacy policy covers what Telegram does with it.
  • Microsoft. The app asks the Microsoft Store whether you're on the free trial or have bought the app. Purchases are handled entirely by Microsoft; the app never sees your payment details.

Nothing is sent to the publisher of this app. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Removing your information

  • Sign out in the app's Settings ends the session with Telegram and deletes it from your PC.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its folder, with your settings, history, chat photos and log.
  • The photos and videos you saved are your files: they stay in their folders until you delete them.

You can also end the app's session from Telegram on your phone: Settings → Devices.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
